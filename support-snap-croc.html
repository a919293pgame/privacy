我買了完整版，換手機之後不見了

打開「設定」分頁，按「回復購買」。一次買斷會跟著你的 Apple ID 走，只要用同一個 Apple ID 登入就能回復，不用再付一次錢。

完整版包含什麼

六組懲罰內容全開、35 種現成懲罰、30 顆與 24 顆牙的難度、以及移除所有廣告。 一次買斷，沒有訂閱。

抽到不想做的懲罰怎麼辦

直接跳過。這是遊戲，不是規定。你也可以在「懲罰包 → 自訂內容」把每一格的文字改成 你們自己的梗，機率也能自己調。

怎麼換語言

「設定 → 語言」可以選中文、English、ไทย。另外還有「客人語言」：打開之後，結果卡會 同時顯示兩種語言，給看不懂你這桌語言的朋友。

為什麼會看到廣告

免費版會在回合之間顯示廣告。購買完整版之後就不會再出現。廣告相關的資料處理寫在 隱私權政策。

Contact us

For questions, bug reports or suggestions, email a919293p@gmail.com and we will get back to you. Telling us your iPhone model and iOS version is the fastest way to get it fixed.

I bought the full version but it is gone on my new phone

Open the Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases. The one-time purchase follows your Apple ID — sign in with the same Apple ID and it comes back at no extra cost.

What is in the full version

All six penalty packs, 35 ready-made penalties, the 30- and 24-tooth difficulties, and no ads. One-time purchase, not a subscription.

What if we do not want to do a penalty

Skip it. This is a game, not a rule book. You can also rewrite every slice under Packs → Custom, and set your own odds while you are there.

Changing language

Settings → Language offers 中文, English and ไทย. There is also a guest language: turn it on and the result card shows both, for the friend who does not read yours.

Why do I see ads

The free version shows an ad between rounds. Buying the full version removes them. How ad data is handled is in the privacy policy.
