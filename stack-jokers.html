English

What we collect

This app collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no ads and no third-party analytics. Your progress, settings, collection and records are stored only on your device, are never transmitted anywhere, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Buying the full version

The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment information. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple ID has bought the full version, and unlocks the content accordingly. The full version can be shared with your family through Apple's Family Sharing.

Children

The app contains no ads and no external links, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we neither collect nor store your personal data, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or delete.
