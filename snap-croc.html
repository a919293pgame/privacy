我們收集什麼

這個 App 本身不收集任何個人資料。 沒有帳號、沒有登入、沒有伺服器。你在遊戲裡設定的

內容（語言、音量、牙齒數、你自己編輯的懲罰文字）全部只存在你的裝置上，不會傳送到任何

地方，刪除 App 時會一併刪除。

廣告（只有免費版）

免費版會在回合之間顯示全螢幕廣告，由 Google AdMob 提供。Google 可能為了提供及

衡量廣告而收集裝置識別碼與廣告互動資料。這部分由 Google 處理，不經過我們的伺服器

（我們沒有伺服器）。

  • Google 的隱私權政策：https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google 如何在廣告中使用資料：https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

第一次開啟時，iOS 會詢問你是否允許追蹤。 你可以拒絕，遊戲功能完全不受影響，只是

廣告不會那麼貼近你的興趣。你也可以隨時在「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」中更改。

歐洲經濟區、英國與瑞士的使用者會另外看到 Google 的同意聲明表單（UMP），依當地法規

詢問廣告資料的使用方式。

購買完整版之後

購買完整版會移除所有廣告，也就不再有任何廣告相關的資料收集。

購買本身完全由 Apple 處理，我們不會看到、也不會儲存你的付款資訊。App 只會向

Apple 詢問「這個 Apple ID 有沒有買過」，並依此解鎖內容。完整版可透過 Apple 的家庭

共享分享給家庭成員。

兒童

本 App 為 17+，內容包含酒精相關的遊戲內容，不適合兒童，也未針對兒童設計。我們

不會刻意收集兒童的任何資料。

English

What we collect

This app collects no personal data of its own. There are no accounts, no sign-in and

no servers. Everything you set in the game — language, volume, tooth count, the penalty

text you write yourself — is stored only on your device, is never transmitted anywhere,

and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a full-screen ad between rounds, served by Google AdMob.

Google may collect device identifiers and ad interaction data in order to deliver and

measure those ads. This is handled by Google and does not pass through our servers (we

have none).

  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data in advertising: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

On first launch, iOS asks whether you allow tracking. You may decline — the game is

unaffected, the ads simply will not match your interests. You can change your choice at

any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are additionally shown Google's consent form

(UMP), which asks about advertising data use as required by local law.

After buying the full version

Buying the full version removes all advertising, and with it any ad-related data

collection.

The purchase itself is handled entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment

information. The app only asks Apple whether this Apple ID has purchased, and unlocks the

content accordingly. The full version can be shared with your family through Apple's

Family Sharing.

Children

This app is rated 17+. It contains alcohol-related game content, is not suitable for

children and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any data from

children.

Your rights

Because we neither collect nor store your personal data, there is nothing for us to

disclose, correct or delete. For advertising data, please refer to Google's policies

linked above.
