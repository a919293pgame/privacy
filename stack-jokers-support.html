I bought the full version but it is gone on my new phone

Open the full-version screen and tap Restore Purchases (恢復購買). The one-time purchase follows your Apple ID — sign in with the same Apple ID and it comes back at no extra cost.

What is in the full version

Levels 1–3 are free to try. The full version unlocks levels 4–9, tougher bosses, and the clear and difficulty badges. One-time purchase, permanent, no subscription, no ads, no consumables.

Will I lose my progress?

The game saves after every move, so you can quit any time. Progress is stored only on your device and is deleted with the app.
